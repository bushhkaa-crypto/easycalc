1. Introduction
Welcome to EasyCalc. Your privacy is important to us. This app is designed to respect and protect your personal information. By using EasyCalc, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
2. Information We Do NOT Collect
EasyCalc does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, or any other personal data.
3. Calculations
All calculations happen only on your device. The numbers you enter are never sent to any server, cloud, or third party. They are not saved after you close the app.
4. Permissions
EasyCalc does not request any device permissions. It does not access your camera, contacts, location, storage, microphone, photos, or any other sensitive data.
5. Data Storage
No data is stored anywhere outside your device. Once you close the app, all entered information is cleared and nothing is saved permanently.
6. Third-Party Services
This app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. There are no SDKs that collect your information.
7. Internet Connection
EasyCalc works completely offline. It does not require an internet connection and does not send any data over the network.
8. Children's Privacy
This app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. Since no data is collected at all, it is safe for users of all ages.
9. Data Security
Since we do not collect any data, there is no risk of data breach or misuse. Your information stays only on your device.
10. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected within the app. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.

Your data never leaves your device.
